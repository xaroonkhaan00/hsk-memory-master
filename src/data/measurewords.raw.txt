# measure word | pinyin | wordId (0 = not a headword) | what it counts | easy rule | examples (zh ~ pinyin ~ english, ;; separated) | common trap
个|gè|32|People, and almost everything else|The safety net. If you cannot remember the right measure word, say 个 — you will be understood.|一个人 ~ yí ge rén ~ one person;;三个苹果 ~ sān ge píngguǒ ~ three apples;;一个问题 ~ yí ge wèntí ~ one question|个 is usually toneless (ge) when counting, not gè. Using 个 everywhere is safe but sounds childish for books (本), cars (辆) and flat things (张).
本|běn|6|Books, magazines, notebooks, dictionaries|Anything BOUND at the spine takes 本.|一本书 ~ yì běn shū ~ one book;;三本词典 ~ sān běn cídiǎn ~ three dictionaries;;这本笔记本 ~ zhè běn bǐjìběn ~ this notebook|Do not use 张 for a book. 张 is for a single flat sheet; a book is many sheets bound, so it is 本.
张|zhāng|580|Flat things with a surface: paper, tables, beds, tickets, photos, maps, faces, cards|If you can spread it out flat, it is 张.|一张纸 ~ yì zhāng zhǐ ~ a sheet of paper;;这张桌子 ~ zhè zhāng zhuōzi ~ this table;;一张信用卡 ~ yì zhāng xìnyòngkǎ ~ one credit card|A chair is NOT 张 — it is 把 (it has a handle/back you grab). Beds and tables are 张.
条|tiáo|521|Long, thin, bendy things: trousers, skirts, roads, rivers, fish, streets, dogs|Long and floppy → 条.|一条裤子 ~ yì tiáo kùzi ~ a pair of trousers;;这条街道 ~ zhè tiáo jiēdào ~ this street;;一条鱼 ~ yì tiáo yú ~ one fish|A shirt (衬衫) is 件, not 条 — it is not long and thin. Trousers and skirts ARE 条.
件|jiàn|197|Upper-body clothes, and abstract "matters"|Tops and troubles: 件 counts shirts, coats — and 事情.|一件衣服 ~ yí jiàn yīfu ~ one item of clothing;;这件衬衫 ~ zhè jiàn chènshān ~ this shirt;;一件重要的事情 ~ yí jiàn zhòngyào de shìqing ~ one important matter|Never 件 for trousers or shoes. Trousers = 条, shoes = 双.
双|shuāng|512|Things that naturally come in twos: shoes, socks, chopsticks, hands, eyes|Two of the same thing that belong together → 双.|一双鞋 ~ yì shuāng xié ~ a pair of shoes;;两双筷子 ~ liǎng shuāng kuàizi ~ two pairs of chopsticks;;一双眼睛 ~ yì shuāng yǎnjing ~ a pair of eyes|A single shoe out of the pair is 一只鞋 (只), not 一双鞋.
只|zhī|584|Animals, birds, and ONE of a pair|Furry, feathered, or half a pair → 只.|一只小猫 ~ yì zhī xiǎo māo ~ one kitten;;一只鸟 ~ yì zhī niǎo ~ one bird;;我有一只鞋找不到了 ~ wǒ yǒu yì zhī xié zhǎo bú dào le ~ I can't find one of my shoes|只 read zhī is a measure word; the SAME character read zhǐ means "only". The tone tells you which one.
位|wèi|534|People you want to be polite to: teachers, guests, customers, managers|The respectful 个. Use 位 for people you address politely.|三位老师 ~ sān wèi lǎoshī ~ three teachers;;这位司机 ~ zhè wèi sījī ~ this driver;;各位乘客 ~ gè wèi chéngkè ~ passengers (announcement)|Do not use 位 for yourself or for children — it sounds odd. Use 个.
口|kǒu|445|People counted as members of a household (and some animals)|Only for "how many people live in this home".|我们家一共三口人 ~ wǒmen jiā yígòng sān kǒu rén ~ there are three of us in the family;;五口人 ~ wǔ kǒu rén ~ five family members|口 does NOT replace 个 for people generally. 三口人 = family size; 三个人 = three people anywhere.
把|bǎ|306|Things you grab by a handle: chairs, umbrellas, knives, keys|If your hand wraps around a handle → 把.|四把椅子 ~ sì bǎ yǐzi ~ four chairs;;一把伞 ~ yì bǎ sǎn ~ one umbrella|把 is also the famous 把-sentence particle (把书放在桌子上). Same character, completely different job.
辆|liàng|456|Vehicles with wheels: cars, bicycles, buses, taxis|Wheels on the road → 辆. The 车 radical is right there in the character.|一辆车 ~ yí liàng chē ~ one car;;这辆自行车 ~ zhè liàng zìxíngchē ~ this bicycle|Planes are 架 and boats are 艘/条 — not 辆. In HSK 3 you only need 辆 for road vehicles.
家|jiā|44|Businesses and institutions: shops, restaurants, hotels, companies, hospitals|A place with a "household" of staff → 家.|一家公司 ~ yì jiā gōngsī ~ one company;;这家超市 ~ zhè jiā chāoshì ~ this supermarket;;一家饭店 ~ yì jiā fàndiàn ~ one restaurant|家 as a measure word never means "home". 我家 = my home; 一家商店 = one shop.
层|céng|331|Floors of a building, and layers|Levels stacked on top of each other → 层.|我住在三层 ~ wǒ zhù zài sān céng ~ I live on the third floor;;这座楼有十层 ~ zhè zuò lóu yǒu shí céng ~ this building has ten floors|楼 also means floor (三楼). 层 counts them; 楼 names them. Both are used.
门|mén|224|School subjects and courses|One door = one subject you walk into.|今年我有五门课 ~ jīnnián wǒ yǒu wǔ mén kè ~ I have five courses this year;;一门外语 ~ yì mén wàiyǔ ~ one foreign language|门 as a measure word has nothing to do with real doors, even though the character is a picture of a door.
路|lù|216|Bus and tram route numbers|The number of the bus line.|坐10路公共汽车 ~ zuò shí lù gōnggòng qìchē ~ take the number 10 bus;;我们可以坐 10 路去 ~ wǒmen kěyǐ zuò shí lù qù ~ we can take the number 10|路 here is not "road". 一条路 = one road; 10路 = the number 10 bus.
站|zhàn|579|Stops along a bus or subway line|Count how many stops, not how far.|坐地铁3站就到了 ~ zuò dìtiě sān zhàn jiù dào le ~ it's three subway stops away;;还有两站 ~ hái yǒu liǎng zhàn ~ two more stops to go|站 is also the verb "to stand" and the noun "station". Context tells you which.
次|cì|162|Times something happens (occurrences)|How many TIMES you did it → 次.|我去过一次北京 ~ wǒ qù guo yí cì Běijīng ~ I have been to Beijing once;;这个药一天吃三次 ~ zhège yào yì tiān chī sān cì ~ take this medicine three times a day|次 goes AFTER the verb: 去过三次, not 三次去过.
回|huí|41|Times something happens (spoken alternative to 次)|Everyday speech uses 回 where writing prefers 次.|我去过一回北京 ~ wǒ qù guo yì huí Běijīng ~ I've been to Beijing once|回 is also the verb "to return" (回家). The measure-word use always follows a number.
块|kuài|51|Yuan in speech; also lumps and pieces|The spoken word for money, and for anything that comes in a chunk.|这本书三十块钱 ~ zhè běn shū sānshí kuài qián ~ this book costs thirty yuan;;来吃块西瓜吧 ~ lái chī kuài xīguā ba ~ come and have a piece of watermelon|块 is spoken; 元 is written. A price tag says 30元, a person says 三十块.
元|yuán|575|Yuan in writing (prices, receipts, bank forms)|The formal, written unit of Chinese money.|这块手表 600 元 ~ zhè kuài shǒubiǎo liùbǎi yuán ~ this watch costs 600 yuan;;这个自行车五百多元 ~ zhège zìxíngchē wǔbǎi duō yuán ~ this bike is over 500 yuan|Never say 元 out loud in a shop unless you are reading a label. Say 块.
角|jiǎo|422|One tenth of a yuan (written); spoken form is 毛|10 角 = 1 元.|桌子上有五角钱 ~ zhuōzi shàng yǒu wǔ jiǎo qián ~ there is fifty cents on the table|角 is written, 毛 is spoken — exactly the same relationship as 元/块.
分|fēn|373|One hundredth of a yuan; also minutes and exam marks|The smallest unit: of money, of an hour, and of a score.|我得了85分 ~ wǒ dé le bāshíwǔ fēn ~ I got 85 marks;;现在8点30分 ~ xiànzài bā diǎn sānshí fēn ~ it is 8:30 now|分 alone = minutes past the hour; 分钟 = a duration of minutes. 我等了三分钟 (I waited three minutes), 三点五分 (5 past 3).
公斤|gōngjīn|384|Kilograms|1 公斤 = 2 斤. Chinese markets use 斤 (500 g), forms use 公斤.|我买了一公斤鸡蛋 ~ wǒ mǎi le yì gōngjīn jīdàn ~ I bought a kilo of eggs;;姐姐只有45公斤 ~ jiějie zhǐ yǒu sìshíwǔ gōngjīn ~ my sister is only 45 kilos|苹果两块钱一斤 means per 500g, not per kilo. Halve the price to compare with home.
米|mǐ|467|Metres|The same character as "rice" — one metre of rice grains laid in a line.|这张桌子有两米长 ~ zhè zhāng zhuōzi yǒu liǎng mǐ cháng ~ this table is two metres long;;有四千多米 ~ yǒu sìqiān duō mǐ ~ over four thousand metres|米饭 is cooked rice; 米 alone in a measurement is always the metre.
岁|suì|103|Years of age|Only for how old someone is.|他儿子今年八岁了 ~ tā érzi jīnnián bā suì le ~ his son is eight this year;;李医生今年40岁 ~ Lǐ yīshēng jīnnián sìshí suì ~ Doctor Li is forty|Never use 年 for age. 三年 = three years long; 三岁 = three years old.
碗|wǎn|529|Bowlfuls of food|The container becomes the counter.|我要一碗面条 ~ wǒ yào yì wǎn miàntiáo ~ I'd like a bowl of noodles;;我吃一碗就可以了 ~ wǒ chī yì wǎn jiù kěyǐ le ~ one bowl is enough for me|Containers double as measure words: 一杯茶 (a cup of tea), 一碗饭 (a bowl of rice), 一盘鱼 (a plate of fish).
杯|bēi|4|Cups and glasses of liquid|The cup counts the drink inside it.|请喝杯茶吧 ~ qǐng hē bēi chá ba ~ please have a cup of tea;;睡觉前喝杯牛奶吧 ~ shuìjiào qián hē bēi niúnǎi ba ~ have a glass of milk before bed|杯子 is the physical cup (a noun); 杯 without 子 is the measure word.
盘|pán|481|Plates and dishes of food|Like 碗, the dish counts what is on it.|盘子里有鱼 ~ pánzi lǐ yǒu yú ~ there is fish on the plate;;一盘菜 ~ yì pán cài ~ one dish of food|盘子 = the object; 盘 = the measure word.
种|zhǒng|589|Kinds, sorts, types|Not "how many" but "how many different kinds".|超市里有很多种新鲜水果 ~ chāoshì lǐ yǒu hěn duō zhǒng xīnxiān shuǐguǒ ~ the supermarket has many kinds of fresh fruit;;这种感冒药 ~ zhè zhǒng gǎnmào yào ~ this kind of cold medicine|种 read zhòng is the verb "to plant". As a measure word it is always zhǒng (third tone).
段|duàn|362|Stretches: of writing, of time, of road|A section cut out of something longer.|这段时间我很忙 ~ zhè duàn shíjiān wǒ hěn máng ~ I've been busy during this period;;一段话 ~ yí duàn huà ~ a passage of speech|段 is for stretches, 条 for the whole long thing: 一条路 (one road) vs 一段路 (a stretch of road).
些|xiē|123|An unspecified small number — "some"|Always follows 一/这/那 and never takes a number bigger than one.|我买了一些苹果 ~ wǒ mǎi le yìxiē píngguǒ ~ I bought some apples;;这些都是新鲜水果 ~ zhèxiē dōu shì xīnxiān shuǐguǒ ~ these are all fresh fruit|You cannot say 三些. Only 一些, 这些, 那些, 哪些.
点儿|diǎnr|131|A small amount — "a bit of"|Use with uncountable things: water, money, time, food.|我只有一点儿钱 ~ wǒ zhǐ yǒu yìdiǎnr qián ~ I only have a little money;;请吃点儿米饭 ~ qǐng chī diǎnr mǐfàn ~ please have some rice|一点儿 goes BEFORE the noun (一点儿水). 有点儿 goes before an adjective and means "annoyingly a bit" (有点儿贵 = a bit too expensive).
号|hào|36|Dates of the month, and numbers of rooms/sizes|Spoken date and any assigned number.|今天是八月九号 ~ jīntiān shì bā yuè jiǔ hào ~ today is the 9th of August;;你的房间号是多少 ~ nǐ de fángjiān hào shì duōshao ~ what is your room number|号 is spoken, 日 is written: a calendar prints 九日, a person says 九号.
