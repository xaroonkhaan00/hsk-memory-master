288,139|char|再 vs 在|在 = WHERE something is (我在家). 再 = AGAIN, and always in the future (再见).
61,218|char|买 vs 卖|卖 has an extra 十 on top — that lid is the shop sign, so goods go OUT. No lid = 买, goods come IN.
104,105,251|char|他 vs 她 vs 它|Same sound tā, different left side: 亻person = he, 女woman = she, 宀roof = it. Nobody can hear the difference; only writing shows it.
15,168,352|grammar|的 vs 得 vs 地|的 + noun (我的书). 得 + how well (跑得快). 地 + verb (慢慢地走).
42,71,209|grammar|会 vs 能 vs 可以|会 = learned skill (我会开车). 能 = physically able right now (我今天不能开车). 可以 = allowed (你可以开我的车).
295,85|meaning|知道 vs 认识|知道 = know a FACT. 认识 = know a PERSON or a character by sight. 我知道他，但是我不认识他。
204,496|meaning|觉得 vs 认为|觉得 = a feeling, softer, personal. 认为 = a considered opinion, used in writing and arguments.
203,327|grammar|就 vs 才|就 = sooner/easier than expected (他七点就来了). 才 = later/harder than expected (他九点才来).
136,139|grammar|有 vs 在|有 puts the THING second (桌子上有书). 在 puts the PLACE second (书在桌子上).
584,585|tone|只 zhī vs 只 zhǐ|Same character, two jobs. zhī (1st tone) = measure word for animals. zhǐ (3rd tone) = only.
187,405|tone|还 hái vs 还 huán|hái = still, yet (他还没来). huán = to give back (我把书还给图书馆).
573,288|grammar|又 vs 再|又 for something that already happened again (他又迟到了). 再 for something that will happen again (明天再来).
278,558|meaning|一起 vs 一共|一起 = together, side by side. 一共 = altogether, a total count.
563,557|meaning|一直 vs 一定|一直 = continuously, the whole time. 一定 = definitely, for sure.
410,395|grammar|或者 vs 还是|Use 还是 when you are ASKING (茶还是咖啡?). Use 或者 when you are TELLING (茶或者咖啡都可以).
256,257|char|完 vs 玩|Identical sound wán. 完 has a roof (宀) = finished. 玩 has jade (王) = playing with a toy.
152,153|char|白 vs 百|百 is 白 with a lid (一) on top: one hundred. Without the lid it is white.
14,106|char|大 vs 太|太 is 大 with a dot underneath. The dot is the extra bit that makes it "too much".
99,204|tone|睡觉 jiào vs 觉得 jué|Same character 觉. In 睡觉 it is jiào (sleep). In 觉得 it is jué (to sense).
565,212|tone|音乐 yuè vs 快乐 lè|Same character 乐. Music = yuè. Happy = lè.
566,551|tone|银行 háng vs 行李 xíng|Same character 行. háng = a line of business (bank). xíng = to go/travel (luggage).
198,424|tone|教室 jiào vs 教 jiāo|jiāo (1st tone) = the act of teaching. jiào (4th tone) = education, as in classroom/teacher.
81,491,237,246|sound|请 / 清楚 / 晴 / 事情|All built on 青 qīng. The left side tells you the meaning: 讠speech=ask, 氵water=clear, 日sun=fine weather, 忄heart=feeling.
95,93,94,505,85|sound|是 shì / 十 shí / 时候 shí / 试 shì / 认识 shí|The shi family. Only the tone separates them: 是 and 试 are 4th tone, 十 时 识 are 2nd tone.
88,115|opposite|上 vs 下|上 = the line ABOVE the base. 下 = the line BELOW. Everything else (上班/下班, 上午/下午, 上车/下车) follows from that picture.

178,303|opposite|高 vs 矮|高 is a tall tower. 矮 has 矢 (a short arrow) — it never grew.
482,506|opposite|胖 vs 瘦|胖 has 月 (flesh) plus 半. 瘦 wears the sickness radical 疒 — too thin.
158,361|opposite|长 vs 短|长 is flowing hair. 短 has 矢, the arrow that fell short.
267,437|opposite|新 vs 旧|新 has 斤 (an axe) — freshly cut. 旧 is worn out. For PEOPLE use 老, never 旧.
211,219|opposite|快 vs 慢|Both have the heart radical 忄. A racing heart is 快; a heavy heart is 慢.
24,90|opposite|多 vs 少|多 is two piles stacked. 少 is 小 with a stroke taken away.

432,405|meaning|借 vs 还|借 covers BOTH borrow and lend — the direction comes from the sentence. 还 (huán) is always giving back.
